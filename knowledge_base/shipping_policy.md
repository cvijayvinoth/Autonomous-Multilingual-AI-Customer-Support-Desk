# Shipping & Delivery Policy

## Domestic Shipping
- Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days across the continental US and major metro areas.
- Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days if orders are placed before 2:00 PM EST.
- Tracking numbers are issued via email within 24 hours of dispatch.

## Delayed Shipments
- If a delivery exceeds 5 business days past the scheduled delivery window, customers are eligible for a full refund of shipping charges and a $10 courtesy credit.
- Lost packages: A shipment is declared officially lost if tracking shows no scan updates for 7 consecutive days. Replacement or full refund is immediately dispatched upon customer confirmation.

## International Shipping
- Takes 7 to 14 business days depending on customs clearance. Import customs duties and taxes are prepaid at checkout.
