# Product FAQ & Warranty Policy

## Hardware Warranty
- All hardware products come with a 1-year limited warranty against manufacturing defects and operational failures.
- Warranty does not cover accidental liquid damage, drop damage, or unauthorized third-party repairs.

## Defective or Damaged Products
- If a product arrives non-functional or broken (e.g., broken blender, cracked screen), we provide a free prepaid shipping label and dispatch a brand-new replacement immediately upon initial carrier scan.
- Customers must provide photos or video demonstration of the defect.
