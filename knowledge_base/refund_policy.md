# Returns, Cancellations & Refund Policy

## Eligibility Window
- Customers can return undamaged, unopened items within 14 calendar days of confirmed delivery.
- Items damaged in transit must be reported with photographic evidence within 48 hours of delivery for immediate replacement or full refund.

## Processing Times & Methods
- Refunds are processed back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days following inspection at our warehouse.
- Store credits are issued instantly and receive an additional 10% bonus credit.

## Non-Returnable Items
- Digital gift cards, downloadable software, personalized goods, and final-sale clearance items cannot be returned or refunded.
