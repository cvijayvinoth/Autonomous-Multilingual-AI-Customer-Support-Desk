# Account Access & Authentication Policy

## Password Recovery
- Users can reset passwords by clicking "Forgot Password" on the login screen. An authentication link valid for 15 minutes is sent to the registered email.
- If the reset email is not received, check the Spam folder or verify if an alternate email was used during registration.

## Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) & Lockouts
- After 5 consecutive failed attempts, accounts are locked for 30 minutes for security purposes.
- If locked out or if you lost access to your 2FA authenticator app, identity verification via photo ID with our Tier-2 security team is required.
